# Stats

Each stat lists what it covers, level anchors, how it behaves over time, and the probes you can use to gather evidence. Levels are absolute rather than graded against people the same age, but call out when someone is ahead of or behind the typical curve for their age.

To score, pick the anchor that the evidence fits best. If the Player lands between two anchors, use the even number between them.

---

## Body
Health, strength, cardio, mobility, sleep, energy.
- **1**: Daily life is physically hard, or a health problem is going unmanaged.
- **3**: Mostly sedentary, low energy, and getting winded easily.
- **5**: Moves regularly, has no major limitations, and sleeps decently.
- **7**: Trains 3 or more times a week and is clearly stronger and fitter than most people their age.
- **9**: Athlete-level conditioning.
- **10**: Competes at an elite level.

**Over time:** fast decay. Weeks off show up within months.
**Probes:** What did a typical week of movement look like this year? How's your sleep? Any injuries or conditions? Could you run a mile or carry groceries up three flights right now?

## Presence (Look & Style)
Grooming, clothing fit, style coherence, posture, and how the Player comes across when they walk into a room.
- **1**: No attention paid to grooming or clothing.
- **3**: Clean and functional, but clothes don't fit well and there's no point of view.
- **5**: Put-together, with clothes that fit and solid grooming.
- **7**: Has a recognizable style that suits them, and carries themselves with confidence.
- **9**: People ask where they got things or copy their look.
- **10**: Their style is a signature people remember.

**Over time:** slow decay, and fast gains. This is often the cheapest stat to raise.
**Probes:** Photos (full body and face, in natural light). What do you wear most days? When did you last update your wardrobe or haircut? How do you want people to read you?

## Mind
Knowledge, learning habit, curiosity, and clear thinking.
- **1**: No learning since school ended.
- **3**: Learns only when forced to.
- **5**: Reads or learns regularly and can explain several topics well.
- **7**: A deep self-directed learner who connects ideas across fields.
- **9**: Others come to them to understand hard things.
- **10**: Produces original thinking that others build on.

**Over time:** slow decay. Learning compounds, because each thing you know makes the next one easier.
**Probes:** What have you learned in the last year that you didn't have to? How do you learn: books, video, doing? What topic could you teach for an hour?

## Craft
The depth of the Player's marketable or professional skill.
- **1**: No developed skill.
- **3**: A beginner or junior who needs supervision.
- **5**: A solidly competent professional.
- **7**: The go-to person on their team or in their scene.
- **9**: Recognized across their field.
- **10**: Top of their field.

**Over time:** compounds, with medium decay if left unused for years.
**Probes:** What do people pay you for or come to you for? How many years have you spent on it, and how many of those were deliberate practice? What's the hardest thing you've done with it?

## Wealth
Money, assets, and financial runway. Measure it in multiples of **annual spending**.
- **1**: Debt is crushing and growing.
- **3**: Living paycheck to paycheck, with little or no savings.
- **5**: Has a 3–6 month emergency fund and is saving or investing regularly.
- **7**: Net worth is at least 3× annual spending.
- **9**: Net worth is at least 12× annual spending.
- **10**: Net worth is at least 25× annual spending, which means financially independent.

**Over time:** compounds. Points spent early are worth the most.
**Probes:** About how much do you spend a year? What's your net worth, roughly (assets minus debts)? Any high-interest debt? What's your income trend? Ranges are fine if exact numbers feel too personal.

## Bonds
Family, partner, friends, and community.
- **1**: Isolated, with no one to call.
- **3**: Has a few relationships, but they're thin, strained, or one-sided.
- **5**: Has a handful of solid people and stable family ties.
- **7**: Has a strong inner circle and is someone others lean on.
- **9**: Anchors a wide, deep network or community.
- **10**: A pillar who holds many lives together.

**Over time:** compounds, with medium decay. Relationships fade without contact.
**Probes:** Who would show up at 2am? How many close friends have you talked to this month? How are things with your family and partner? Are you part of any group or community?

## Creation
Finished things in the world that exist without the Player standing next to them: music, businesses, art, code, writing.
- **1**: Nothing made.
- **3**: Has made things, but mostly unfinished or private.
- **5**: Has a finished, public body of work.
- **7**: Others use, buy, or regularly engage with the work.
- **9**: The work has a significant audience or impact.
- **10**: The work defines or shifts its space.

**Over time:** no decay. Finished work stays finished, and some of it keeps compounding.
**Probes:** What have you finished and released? What's sitting at 80% done? Who uses or listens to it?

## Spirit
Purpose, mental health baseline, self-knowledge, and resilience.
- **1**: In crisis. If this is the read, follow ground rule 6.
- **3**: Mostly drifting, often anxious or numb.
- **5**: Generally okay, with some sense of direction.
- **7**: Clear on their values, grounded, and recovers from setbacks well.
- **9**: Deeply at peace, and steady for others too.
- **10**: Unshakeable equanimity.

**Over time:** varies. Practices like therapy, faith or reflection compound; neglect erodes it quietly.
**Probes:** How do you feel most mornings? What do you care about most? What do you do when life knocks you down? When did you last feel fully yourself?

---

## Starting roll (not scored)
Record genetics and health at birth, family resources, place and era, and early advantages or obstacles. Use these to explain efficiency, not to judge it.

## Custom stats
If the Player adds a stat, work with them to define it and write anchors for 1, 3, 5, 7, 9 and 10 before scoring it.
