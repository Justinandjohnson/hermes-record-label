# What this method is built on

Each mechanic in the skill borrows from a method with real evidence behind it. Use this file to explain *why* a step exists when the Player asks.

| Mechanic | Source | What we take from it |
|---|---|---|
| Origin story: chapters plus key scenes | Dan McAdams, *The Life Story Interview* (Northwestern Foley Center) | Life told as a book. Divide it into chapters, then ask for a high point, low point, turning point, and future script. How someone tells a story is evidence too. **Redemption** stories ("the bad thing led somewhere good") go with better adaptation. **Contamination** stories ("a good thing got ruined") go with worse. |
| Interview style | Motivational Interviewing, OARS: Open questions, Affirmations, Reflections, Summaries | People commit to change they talk themselves into, not change they get lectured into. When a Player says on their own that they want to change something (**change talk**), that predicts actual change. |
| Distrust hour estimates | Robinson's time-diary studies, and Laura Vanderkam's *168 Hours* | People overstate busy hours, and the bigger the claim, the bigger the overstatement. People claiming 60–64 hour workweeks averaged about 44 in their diaries. So anchor on a specific recent week, and use a real time log for the present. |
| Points ≠ level (efficiency) | Macnamara et al. 2014, a meta-analysis of deliberate practice | On average, practice explained about 14% of the variance in performance: 26% in games, 21% in music, 18% in sports, 4% in education, and under 1% in professions. Hours matter, but the starting roll, practice quality, and leverage matter too. That's why efficiency is a separate stat. |
| Importance × consistency gap | Valued Living Questionnaire, from Acceptance and Commitment Therapy (ACT) | Rate how much each area matters (1–10) and how well last week's actions matched it (1–10). The biggest gap is the biggest leverage. |
| Dashboard snapshot | *Designing Your Life* (Burnett & Evans): the Health / Work / Play / Love dashboard | A quick "you are here" gauge before diving deep. |
| Alternate builds | *Designing Your Life*: Odyssey Plans | Sketch three different 5-year versions of the Player before committing to a respec. |
| Quests, power-ups, bad guys, allies | Jane McGonigal, *SuperBetter* | A gameful frame for real goals. Randomized trials (University of Pennsylvania; an NIH-funded trial at Ohio State and Cincinnati Children's) showed better mood and self-efficacy. Every quest gets an ally. |
| Quest planning | WOOP / MCII (Oettingen and Gollwitzer). Meta-analysis: Wang et al. 2021, g ≈ 0.34 across about 15,900 people | Wish → Outcome → Obstacle → Plan, with the plan written as "if X, then Y." The effect was stronger when coached live (g ≈ 0.47) than when done from a worksheet, which is why we do it in conversation. |
| Weighting Bonds | Harvard Study of Adult Development (Waldinger) | Relationship quality predicts long-term health and happiness better than wealth, fame, IQ or genes. When Bonds is low, always name it, even if the Player ranks it low in importance. Let them decide what to do with that. |
