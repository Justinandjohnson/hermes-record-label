# Points math

**1 point = 100 hours** of time actually spent on a stat.

## Annual budget

Use these defaults unless the Player gives their real numbers:

| Item | Hours/day | Hours/year | Points/year |
|---|---|---|---|
| Waking time (8h sleep) | 16 | 5,840 | 58 |
| Maintenance (eating, hygiene, chores, errands, commute) | 3 | 1,095 | 11 |
| **Spendable** | 13 | 4,745 | **~47** |

- Paid work is spendable time. Allocate it to whatever it actually built, usually Craft and Wealth, and sometimes Bonds or Mind.
- Passive time (scrolling, background TV) still counts as spent, but it builds almost nothing. Log it in an **Idle** column so the Player can see the leak. Idle is not a stat and has no level.
- If the Player sleeps 6 hours or commutes 2, recompute their budget. Always show the math you used.

## Who controlled the points

| Age | Allocation |
|---|---|
| 0–5 | Not counted. These years are part of the starting roll. |
| 6–17 | Count the points, but tag them **guided**: school, parents and circumstance mostly decided where they went. |
| 18+ | Count the points as **chosen**. |

Showing guided and chosen points separately is the honest way to say "you didn't pick everything."

## Converting weekly hours

`points for a chapter = weekly hours × 52 × years in chapter ÷ 100`

For example, 10 hours a week of guitar for 4 years gives 10 × 52 × 4 ÷ 100 ≈ **21 points** in Creation.

## Points remaining

`remaining = (horizon age − current age) × spendable points per year`

State the horizon and where it came from: the Player's own choice, or a life-expectancy figure looked up and cited. Do not make up a life-expectancy number.

## Energy and decay

- Later decades don't always offer the same energy. If the Player wants the adjustment, ask how they expect their capacity to change. Don't apply a made-up multiplier.
- **Decay** happens to stat levels, not points. Points spent are history and never disappear. A level can drop when upkeep stops; see the decay notes in `stats.md`.
