# Age-group benchmarks

These are real US figures for comparing a Player to their peers. When you use one, cite the year it comes from. If a stat has no benchmark here, say "no benchmark." Never estimate a peer figure. If the Player is outside the US, or a newer figure is needed, look it up with WebSearch and cite it.

When comparing, say **where the Player sits**, for example "above the median for men 18–29." Don't turn that into a percentile unless the source actually gives percentiles.

| Stat | Benchmark | Figure | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| Body | VO2max, men 20–29 (ml/kg/min) | 5th 29.0 · 25th 40.1 · **50th 48.0** · 75th 55.2 · 95th 66.3 | FRIEND registry (Kaminsky 2015, treadmill). The 2022 update runs 1.5–4.6 lower. |
| Body | VO2max, men 30–39 (ml/kg/min) | 5th 27.2 · 25th 35.9 · **50th 42.4** · 75th 49.2 · 95th 59.8 | Same source |
| Wealth | Median household net worth, head of household under 35 | **$39,040** (mean $183,500) | Federal Reserve SCF 2022 |
| Wealth | Median household net worth, ages 35–44 | **$135,300** | Federal Reserve SCF 2022 |
| Craft / Wealth | Median full-time weekly earnings, ages 25–34 | **$1,140**/wk (about $59k/yr). Men: $1,199/wk (about $62k/yr). | BLS, Q2 2025 |
| Mind | Bachelor's degree or higher, ages 25–29 | **40%** | NCES, 2022 |
| Bonds | Men reporting **no** close friends | **15%** (up from 3% in 1990) | Survey Center on American Life, 2021 |
| Bonds | Single (no marriage, cohabiting partner or committed relationship), ages 18–29 | Men **63%**, women 34% | Pew Research, 2022 |

## The "by 30" checkpoint
Many players treat 30 as the end of Act 1, the point by which a path should be set. When a Player picks 30 as a checkpoint, show:
- **points left until 30**, as `(30 − age) × spendable points per year`,
- where they sit against the under-35 and 25–34 benchmarks above.
