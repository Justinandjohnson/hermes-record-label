# Interview guide

The interview collects **every fact before any analysis happens**. Run it in **rounds**. Each round is one batch of questions that the Player can answer in a single long reply, in any order. Typed or dictated is fine.

## Rules for the interviewer
- **Collect, don't conclude.** During the interview, don't give scores, archetypes, hypotheses or peer comparisons. Reflections are factual summaries only. All analysis waits for the snapshot (SKILL.md, Phase 3).
- **One round per turn.** Open each round with a single sentence on why it matters, then list the numbered questions.
- **Gaps roll forward.** After each reply, add any unanswered or vague items to the next round as **follow-ups**, at most 5. A round counts as closed when every item is answered or marked *unknown* or *declined*.
- **Opinion breaks on request.** If the Player asks for your honest take in the middle of the interview, give it: blunt, specific, and tied to evidence. Don't pad it with empathy when they've asked for none. Label it as a read on partial data, and give no scores, levels or archetypes, since those wait for the snapshot. Then return to the current round.
- **Follow the Player's shape.** Some Players answer by theme across several chapters instead of chapter by chapter. Tag each fact with its chapter in the log. Then ask a later chapter's round for **its gaps only**, so nothing already answered gets asked again.
- **Unknown is not zero.** "I don't know" gets recorded as *unknown*. Never fill a gap with an assumption.
- **Specifics beat summaries.** When the Player gives a general claim ("I'm better than most at anything"), ask for 2–3 concrete instances that show it. The claim gets logged as a claim. The instances get logged as evidence.
- **Evidence strength, strongest first:**
  1. Records and numbers: statements, screen-time reports, test results, user or revenue counts, photos.
  2. Specific episodes, with a when, a who and a what.
  3. Outside view: what other people have said.
  4. Self-description.

  Each score's confidence (High, Med or Low) follows the strongest evidence it rests on.
- **Save after every round.** Write the answers into the save file's *Interview log* section, condensed but faithful. Use the Player's own words for anything emotional, any change talk, and any claims.

## Round 1: Starting roll
*Why: to separate the dice you were handed from the points you spent.*
1. When and where were you born? Where did you grow up, and how many times did you move?
2. Who raised you? What did they do for work, and what was the relationship like between them?
3. Do you have siblings? What was the birth order, and how are those relationships now?
4. Money growing up: struggling, getting by, comfortable, or well-off?
5. What was home like? Warm, strict, chaotic, absent, safe? Give one memory that sums it up.
6. Any health issues, disabilities, or physical traits that shaped your childhood?
7. What kind of schools did you go to, and how did school go for you?
8. Describe yourself as a kid in three words. Then describe how adults described you.
9. Any big early events: a loss, a divorce, a move, an illness, a windfall?

## Round 2: Chapter map
*Why: to build the skeleton of the whole story before filling it in.*
1. If your life were a book, what are the chapters? Usually 5–8 is right. For each one, give a title, the age range, and one line on what it was about.
2. Which chapter are you in now, and when did it start?

If the Player can't name chapters, they tell the timeline in their own words. The GM then drafts the chapter map from those facts: a title, an age range and one line per chapter, with each break placed at a real turning point. The Player corrects the draft before it's locked. Any ages or facts that conflict go into follow-ups. Never fix a conflict by guessing.

## Rounds 3 to N: One round per chapter
*Why: each answer feeds one stat's column in the point ledger.* Ask the same set every time, so no chapter gets skipped on any stat.
1. **Setting.** Where did you live, who with, and what were you doing (school, job, other)?
2. **A real day.** Walk me through a normal weekday and a normal weekend day from back then, hour by hour as best you remember.
3. **Body.** Sports, gym, activity, sleep, health. What shape were you in?
4. **Mind.** What were you learning, and how? Grades, self-taught, obsessions?
5. **Craft and Wealth.** Jobs, skills you were building, money coming in and going out, any debt.
6. **Charisma and Bonds.** Who were your people (rough count and names)? How were you socially? Family relationships? Dating?
7. **Creation.** What did you make, and what did you actually finish?
8. **Presence.** How did you look and dress, and how did you feel about it?
9. **Spirit.** Your general mood, what you cared about, and the hardest thing you dealt with.
10. **Discipline.** What did you stick with, and what did you quit?
11. **Bookends.** What was the biggest win and the biggest loss? What ended this chapter?

## Round: Key scenes
*Why: these are the moments the story hangs on, and the source of traits.* For each one, give what happened, who was there, what you felt, and what it changed.
1. High point: the best moment of your life so far.
2. Low point: the worst.
3. Turning point: the moment your direction changed.
4. Earliest memory you'd count as important.
5. A vivid scene from childhood, one from adolescence, and one from adulthood.
6. A moment of wisdom: when you, or someone else, handled something really well.
7. The relationship scene: the moment that best captures your longest relationship, good or bad.

## Round: Now, in hard numbers
*Why: today's levels get scored from facts, not vibes.* Ranges are fine, and anything can be declined.
- **Body:** height, weight, and waist if you know it. Resting heart rate and VO2max if a watch tracks them. Workouts in the last 7 days. Mile or 5k time, or "can't run one." Average sleep. Any conditions, medications or injuries. Alcohol, nicotine or other substances, and how often.
- **Mind:** formal education. What you learned in the last 12 months. Three topics you could teach for an hour.
- **Discipline:** every project you've started in the last 24 months, with its status: shipped, live and maintained, paused, or abandoned. Your longest-ever habit streak. What you do every single day without fail.
- **Charisma:** how a conversation with a stranger usually goes. Dates in the last 12 months. The last conflict you handled, and how it went. Feedback you've gotten about how you come across. How comfortable you are in groups and speaking in public.
- **Spirit:** how most mornings feel. Any history of anxiety or depression, and any support you've had. Practices like therapy, faith, journaling or meditation. Your top three values.
- **Craft:** what you do for money now, and who pays you for what. Proof of skill: clients, revenue, users, roles, recognition.
- **Wealth:** income in the last 12 months. What you spend in a year. Assets and debts, giving net worth. Any high-interest debt. Whether your income is going up or down.
- **Bonds:** the names of everyone who would show up at 2am. Non-work people you talked to in the last 30 days. Where things stand with each family member. Any community you belong to. Your romantic status and history.
- **Creation:** everything you've shipped, with users, listeners or revenue for each. Everything sitting at 80% done.
- **Presence:**
  - Your grooming routine.
  - How old your wardrobe is.
  - How you want people to read you.
  - **Photos**, all in natural light, no filters:
    1. Face, from the front
    2. Full body standing relaxed, from the front
    3. Full body from the side
    4. Your everyday outfit
    5. Your best outfit
    6. A recent candid photo someone else took

## Round: The real week
*Why: memory overstates busy hours, and logs don't.*
1. Walk me through last Tuesday, hour by hour.
2. Walk me through last Saturday, hour by hour.
3. Your phone's weekly screen-time report: the total and the top 5 apps. Screenshot or numbers.

## Round: Outside view (optional, and strong evidence)
*Why: everyone rates themselves with blind spots.*
1. Ask 2–3 people who know you (family, coworkers, an ex) for three words that describe you. Report back exactly what they said.
2. Any written feedback: performance reviews, testimonials, customer comments, dating-app feedback.

## Round: The future
*Why: the respec needs a destination.*
1. What's the next chapter supposed to be?
2. What do you want to be true at your next checkpoint, for example 35 or 40?
3. If nothing changed, what would you regret most at 80?
4. If what you do now vanished tomorrow, what would you do instead?
5. If money and other people's opinions didn't matter, what would you do?
6. If a checkpoint has already passed (like 30 for a Player who's 33), what did you expect to have by then, and what's actually there?

## Completeness gate
Do not start the snapshot until every item here is ✅ or explicitly marked *unknown* or *declined*:
- [ ] Starting roll: all 9 items
- [ ] A chapter map that covers age 0 to now with no gaps
- [ ] All 11 questions answered for every chapter
- [ ] All key scenes
- [ ] The hard-numbers round, with at least 2 evidence items per stat that are stronger than self-description
- [ ] Photos (or declined)
- [ ] The real week: both days
- [ ] The future round
- [ ] Every general claim has concrete instances, or is logged as unverified

Show the gate checklist to the Player whenever they ask "where are we?"
